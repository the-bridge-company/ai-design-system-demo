The sample team runs the code of any team on Home, the review queue, the product page, the Library and Settings: no separate demo home, persona Members or sample Settings. Its visitor (Dana, an email admin) gets the lead home and its tour, the real members, review rules, products, Danger zone and settings of the team, and every write is refused by the server in the demo dialog's words (403 `demo`), now on the invites and entry servers too. The team's name comes from its `bridge.config.json` like any team's.
