DS Bridge is installed from Figma Community. The Install page (Figma plugin) and /docs/figma-plugin/ send designers to the Community page (https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1693034743850648023/ds-bridge): Open in, then Plugins > DS Bridge, in the desktop app or the browser, with updates from Figma. The zip becomes the development build for unpublished changes. Settings shows "Community page" without the "in review" tag.
