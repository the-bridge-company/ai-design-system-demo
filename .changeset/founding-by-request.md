Bridge on invitation, PR C: the founding offer follows the date of the access request, not of team creation. Terms version 3 (effective 24 November 2026): a founding team is any team created before version 3, the first team of a person whose access request is sent by 31 March 2027, or of a person who joins through an invite link created by then; a founding place lapses if the invite is not accepted within 30 days, and people invited into a founding team are covered by it (10.1, 10.8). The strip, the homepage founding section, /pricing/ (hero, Free and Team cards, founding band, FAQ), the comparison and docs/BILLING.md say "request access by 31 March 2027". A request sent by the cutoff (Paris day) shows "Your founding place is saved." on /request/, in "Your Bridge request is in" with its date, and "Accept within 30 days to keep your founding place." in the invite email. AnnouncementStrip examples use the same sentence.
