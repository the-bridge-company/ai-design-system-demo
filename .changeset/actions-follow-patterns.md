Bridge's own screens follow its Action patterns. Delete this team and Delete my account ask in an AlertDialog where
the person types the team's name or their email. Removing a Claude connection, removing someone from a team and
discarding draft changes ask in a plain AlertDialog, never window.confirm. Removing a recent team, archiving an
exploration and closing a change act at once, and their toast offers Undo.
