Delete pattern: plain confirmation, or the name typed to confirm when the impact is high (a team, an account, anything other people lose), with the AlertDialog's `confirmText`. A pattern stage can name its high impact rows (`stage.typeName`: `items` and their own question); `validatePattern` checks they are rows of the stage and that the action cannot be undone. The Library's Delete stage plays both: Marketing team asks for its name, the two drafts ask a plain question.
