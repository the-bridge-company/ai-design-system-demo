The illustrated face's hair styles, glasses and beards have human names. `FACE_PART_NAMES` and `faceVariantName(part, variant)` give each of the 64 hair styles, 11 glasses and 12 beards a short name written from its drawing ("Short curls", "Space buns", "Round glasses", "Handlebar moustache"), unique within its part. They are exported by `@bridge-ds/react/avatar-illustrated` and by the new data-only entry `@bridge-ds/react/avatar-illustrated/names`, which a page or a server imports without loading the drawings.

Settings > You > Your face names its tiles with them ("Curly puffs, taken by Sofia" instead of "Hair style 40, taken by Sofia") and says them after a Shuffle ("New face: space buns, round sunglasses."). Hat, No glasses and No beard stay.
