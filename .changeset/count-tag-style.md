One count tag, legible: Badge takes `appearance="count"` for a number next to its label. Ink figures (text.default, medium, tabular) on the emphasis neutral fill, no border, at 7:1 or more in every brand and theme; inside the current nav item, the selected tab or the checked segment it takes the stronger tone (surface.inverse). Tabs, SegmentedControl and SidebarNav now render their `count` with it, so the three and every Studio count (side nav, group counts, Library review, page tabs, list filters) share one style. Before, counts were muted text on the neutral status fill with a hairline border (5.08:1) and read as disabled.
