Studio's header search finds the team's work, not only its components and tokens. Results are grouped by kind: Explorations (by name, product or person, with the list's status and who explores it, "In review · Sofia"), the pieces waiting in Library review that the viewer acts on (with the list's state words, "New size · Waiting 2 days"), the build requests not shipped yet on Being built, the team's products, components, tokens, then Studio's pages (Settings, Members, Quality...) under their former names too. Components, tokens, products and pages show at once; explorations, reviews and build requests come from the same reads Studio's pages make, the first time the palette opens, while a loading row says so. The search button and field now say "Search explorations, components, tokens".
