Settings > You, Your face: someone with a GitHub photo now picks Shown as, Photo or Drawn face. The photo stays the default. Drawn face is saved on their account at once, so it follows them in every team: the account menu, the side nav, rows, greetings and people lists draw that face instead of the photo, for them and for their teammates. Change comes back to customise it, and Photo goes back to the photo while keeping the drawn face for later.
