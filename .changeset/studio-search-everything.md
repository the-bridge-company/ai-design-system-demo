Studio's header search finds the team's work, not only its components and tokens. Results are grouped by kind: Explorations (by name, product or person, with the same status word as the Explorations list, read from the same task and review reads once they are in, and who explores it by first name, "In review · Sofia"), the pieces waiting in Library review that the viewer acts on (with the list's state words, "New size · Waiting 2 days"), the build requests not shipped yet on Being built, the team's products, components, tokens, then Studio's pages under their former names too, and the Settings bands (Members, Review rules, Figma and Claude) that this Studio's Settings page has. Components, tokens, products and pages show at once; explorations, reviews and build requests come from the same reads Studio's pages make, the first time the palette opens, while a loading row says so. The search button and field now say "Search explorations, components, tokens".
