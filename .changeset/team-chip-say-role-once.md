Studio says the person's role once. When the account row at the foot of the side nav already names it (a Bridge account on this team, an email member, the sample team's developer), the team chip shows only the team's name; it keeps its badge when it says something the row does not (Checking, Explore freely, Guest, a visitor, signed out). The teams list in the chip's menu keeps every role. On the sample team, "See it as" now names what the person played does ("Dana Design lead", "Malik Developer"), and its face, words and chevron sit on one axis.
